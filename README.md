# DES646-Project-Deepfake-Guard

With the rapid advancement of AI-generated media, deepfake videos have emerged as a major threat to digital authenticity and information integrity. Deepfake Guard aims to detect and prevent synthetic manipulations in video content through a combination of advanced computer vision, biometric pattern recognition, and behavioral analysis. Our objective is to develop a system that can identify manipulated facial, audio, and environmental cues with high precision.
